Fundraising

Fifty Hounds is an independent short film lead by a predominantly female team. Every contribution—whether through funding, sharing, or simply following our progress—helps us bring this story to life.

We are raising funds to support production—helping us secure equipment, transportation, and compensate our crew.

Our current goal is to raise our skeleton budget of $25,000.

This includes reduced rates for cast and crew, along with deferred payment for our co-creators.

Financing secured over the skeleton budget—from grants and crowdfunding—will allow us to pay our crew their full asking rates, cover writer/director Halle Skye's travel and directorial rate, and make more artistically ambitious choices with our project.

Stories have the power to broaden perspectives and challenge the systems that shape our world. With this film, we reimagine ancient narratives surrounding the inherent powers of womanhood, using them as a lens to spark conversation about female autonomy—over one’s body and one’s future.

Attached Production Companies

286 PRODUCTIONS

286 PRODUCTIONS is a female-run, artist-first production company championing bold, resonant storytelling through female perspectives while cultivating creative community. Founded by Writer/Director Carley Lovito, 286 has lead works including the world premiere of Clifford Odets' The Nursery, The award winning film ‘Everything I Never Told You' and up and coming films including ‘Can't You See I'm Trying', ‘Holy Water', ‘Peach Fuzz' and ‘It Girl'.

Rise studios

RISE STUDIOS is the production branch of RISE FLIX that handles the distribution company’s production services from sourcing financing, to leveraging partnerships with rental houses for equipment, to building cost-effective professional crews from their filmmaker network, to sourcing name talent through their industry network of talent representatives and casting directors. They produce narrative films, short proof-of-concepts, and feature films.

Budget Breakdown

Camera

Covers the rental and maintenance of our shooting camera, lenses, and essential accessories needed to achieve the visual language of the film. This ensures high-quality image capture that supports the project’s aesthetic and technical goals.

Crew

Allocates funds for key production personnel, including the director of photography, sound mixer, and more. Compensating crew fairly supports a professional working environment and ensures efficiency on set.

Art Department

Supports production design needs such as set dressing, props, and visual details that establish the film’s world. These elements are essential for conveying tone, character, and narrative authenticity—especially in a period piece.

Equipment

Includes lighting, sound, and other rentals required for a safe and effective production. Proper equipment allows the crew to work efficiently while maintaining consistent visual and audio quality.

Post Production

Covers editing, sound design, color correction, and final mastering. This stage shapes the raw footage into a cohesive finished film and is critical to the project’s overall impact.

Cast

Provides compensation for actors and performers. Funding cast ensures committed performances and allows the production to work with talent who can bring depth and credibility to the story.

Food/Transport

Accounts for meals, craft services, and transportation for cast and crew during production. These essentials maintain morale, energy, and punctuality on set.

Contingency

Reserved for unexpected expenses or production challenges. This buffer helps keep the project on schedule and prevents unforeseen costs from disrupting completion.
