Fundraising

Fifty Hounds is an independent short film led by a predominantly female lead team. Every contribution — whether through funding, sharing, or simply following our progress — helps us bring this story to life.

We are raising funds to support production, helping us secure equiptment, transportation, and compensate our crew. By weaving together myth and film, we aim to start greater conversations surrounding the necessity of female autonomy over one's future and body.
